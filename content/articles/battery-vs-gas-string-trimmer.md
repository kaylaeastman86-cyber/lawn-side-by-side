## The short answer

If you trim one average yard, a battery trimmer is the easier choice. It starts instantly, it's lighter, and there's no fuel to store. Pick gas if you have a large property, thick weeds along fence lines, or you trim for hours at a time.

## Side by side

| | Battery | Gas |
|---|---|---|
| Startup | Push a trigger | Pull cord, choke, prime |
| Weight | Usually lighter | Usually heavier |
| Runtime | Limited by battery | As long as you have fuel |
| Thick weeds | Fine for most yards | Better for heavy brush |
| Upkeep | Charge and store | Fuel mix, spark plug, air filter |

## Pick battery if…

- You trim one home yard
- You already own batteries from the same brand
- You want something quiet and easy to start

## Pick gas if…

- You have a large lot or acreage
- You cut thick weeds and overgrowth often
- You'd rather refuel than wait on a charger

## From the field

> Line matters as much as the trimmer. A heavier, quality line lasts longer along concrete edges than the thin line that comes in the box.
