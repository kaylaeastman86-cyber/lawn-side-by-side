## The short answer

For most homeowners with a quarter acre or less, a good cordless blower is the better buy. It starts instantly, it's quieter, and there's no fuel to mix or store over winter. Gas still wins for big lots and heavy, wet leaves.

## Side by side

| | Cordless | Gas |
|---|---|---|
| Startup | Push a button | Pull cord, choke, prime |
| Noise | Quieter, neighbor-friendly | Loud, ear protection needed |
| Runtime | Limited by battery | As long as you have fuel |
| Upkeep | Charge and go | Fuel mix, plugs, filters |
| Best for | Small to mid yards, decks, driveways | Big lots, wet matted leaves |

## Pick cordless if…

- Your yard is under about a quarter acre
- You already own batteries from the same tool brand
- Noise rules or early-morning neighbors matter to you

## Pick gas if…

- You clear an acre or more, or lots of wet leaves
- You need to run it for an hour or more at a time
- You don't mind basic small-engine upkeep

## From the field

> Wet leaves are the real test. Clear them while they're dry if you can; even a strong blower struggles once leaves are soaked and stuck to the grass.
