## The short answer

Run hi-lift blades when you bag or need strong discharge in tall, thick grass. Run mulching blades when you're recycling clippings on accounts mowed often enough that clippings stay short.

## Side by side

| | Hi-lift | Mulching |
|---|---|---|
| Airflow | High, strong lift | Lower, cuts clippings multiple times |
| Bagging | Excellent | Poor |
| Clippings | Discharged or bagged | Chopped fine and dropped |
| Tall or wet grass | Handles it better | Can clump |
| Best for | Bagging accounts, overgrown lawns | Weekly accounts, clean finish |

## Run hi-lift if…

- You bag on most accounts
- You're cutting overgrown or thick grass
- You want a clean, strong discharge

## Run mulching if…

- Your accounts are mowed weekly
- Clients want clippings left on the lawn
- You want fewer clippings to blow off hardscapes

## From the field

> Keep a sharpened backup set on the trailer. Swapping a dull set takes minutes and keeps cut quality up for the rest of the day.
