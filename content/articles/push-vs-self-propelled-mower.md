## The short answer

For a small, flat yard, a push mower does the job and costs less. If your yard has hills, is on the larger side, or you just want mowing to feel easier, self-propelled is worth paying more for.

## Side by side

| | Push | Self-propelled |
|---|---|---|
| Effort | You push the full weight | Drive wheels do the work |
| Slopes | Tiring on hills | Much easier on hills |
| Price | Lower | Higher |
| Parts to wear | Fewer | Drive belt and cables to maintain |
| Best for | Small, flat yards | Larger yards, slopes |

## Pick push if…

- Your yard is small and mostly flat
- You want the lowest price and simplest machine
- You don't mind a little workout

## Pick self-propelled if…

- Your yard has slopes or is larger
- Mowing wears you out
- You bag clippings often, since a full bag adds weight

## From the field

> Whatever you buy, keep the blade sharp. A dull blade tears grass and leaves brown tips, and no mower upgrade fixes that.
