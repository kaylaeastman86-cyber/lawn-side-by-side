## The short answer

If your storms are usually light and your driveway is paved, a single-stage blower is lighter, cheaper and easier to store. If you get deep snow, heavy drifts, or have a gravel driveway, go two-stage.

## Side by side

| | Single-stage | Two-stage |
|---|---|---|
| How it works | Auger scoops and throws | Auger feeds an impeller that throws |
| Snow depth | Light to moderate | Deep and heavy |
| Gravel drives | Not recommended | Safe, auger sits above the ground |
| Size and storage | Compact | Large, needs garage space |
| Best for | Paved driveways, sidewalks | Long drives, heavy snow regions |

## Pick single-stage if…

- Most storms bring light to moderate snow
- Your driveway and walks are paved
- Storage space is tight

## Pick two-stage if…

- You regularly get deep or wet, heavy snow
- You have a gravel driveway
- You clear a long drive or the plow pile at the end

## From the field

> Treat it like any small engine before the first storm: fresh fuel, check the oil, and make sure the shear pins are in good shape so you aren't stuck mid-storm.
